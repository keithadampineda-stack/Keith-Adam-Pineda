Discover Cebu's Mountains

Cebu is not only known for its beaches and cities. Its mountains also offer beautiful views, challenging trails, and peaceful places for hikers.

This website features three hiking destinations near Cebu City: Mount Kan-Irag Campsite, Bocaue Peak, and the 6 Towers Trail. Each destination offers a different hiking experience depending on your fitness level and time available.

Before hiking, always check the weather and trail conditions, bring enough water, and prepare the proper equipment.

Mount Kan-Irag Campsite

Mount Kan-Irag

LOCATION Cebu
DIFFICULTY Moderate
DISTANCE 6.9 km
TIME 2.5–3 hrs

Mount Kan-Irag is a popular hiking destination near Cebu City. The trail is a good choice for hikers looking for a shorter adventure and beautiful views of the city and sea.

Bocaue Peak

Bocaue Peak

LOCATION Central Cebu
DIFFICULTY Hard
DISTANCE 12.7 km
TIME 4.5–5 hrs

Bocaue Peak is a challenging hiking route located within the Central Cebu Protected Landscape. The trail requires more time and preparation but rewards hikers with scenic mountain views.

6 Towers Trail

6 Towers Trail

LOCATION Buhisan Watershed
DIFFICULTY Hard
DISTANCE 13.7 km
TIME 5.5–6 hrs

The 6 Towers Trail is the longest of the three featured hikes. It passes through the Buhisan Watershed Forest Reserve and is best suited for hikers who are prepared for a full-day adventure.
